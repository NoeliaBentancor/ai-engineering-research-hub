Forward-Deployed Engineering Philosophy

My approach to forward-deployed AI engineering emphasizes:

  • Practical Integration: AI solutions that seamlessly integrate with existing development workflows
  • Transparency: Clear understanding of how agents make decisions and when they might fail
  • Iterative Development: Continuous improvement through feedback loops and real-world usage
  • Collaboration: Agents that work alongside human engineers rather than replacing them
